What's new on top of 8c

  • Steps read like what they do: keys as keycaps, clicks and checks with a thumbnail of their target (and a check's score), waits with a bar as long as the wait.
  • Sections: each has its own colour, a glowing dot and a small progress bar while playing. Folding slides the rows closed.
  • Play: a green playhead walks the steps and the list follows. Finished steps dim, and a breakpoint flashes red and stops it.
  • Motion: settings and Peek unfold, rows slide in, and the breadcrumb animates when you open a call. With reduced-motion turned on, it all becomes plain fades.
  • Drag to reorder: the row lifts as a ghost, and a glowing line shows where it lands.
