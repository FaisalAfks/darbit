Studio round 3: the look

The same Overview page in three looks. The layout follows round 2: the bot dropdown on the left (a sidebar is a setting), the page bar in the centre, and the bot's controls on the right (Play, Pause, Stop, then Edit). Motion is at the bottom: open the HTML and press the buttons.

1Glass

The look of the approved mockup: see-through panels over a soft colour wash (later, the bot's last screenshot, blurred). It's the most "wow". Blur costs while resizing, so it's kept to the panels and never put on the whole page; that also fixes the resize lag.
