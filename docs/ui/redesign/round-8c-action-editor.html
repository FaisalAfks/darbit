Studio round 8c: a calmer action editor

One wide list and nothing else on screen. Open the HTML and try it:

  • section headers stick to the top while you scroll, and fold when clicked;
  • Jump to lists every section;
  • click a step to open its settings under it, and click again to close them;
  • hover between two rows for ＋ to insert a step;

▶❚❚■✎ Editing

Only the open action's steps are on screen. Calls stay one line until you peek into them. Settings appear only for the step you're working on. Multi-select (Shift/Ctrl) and bulk edits from 8b stay: the bulk buttons show in the header while several steps are selected.

What changed from 8b

  • No timeline (it can come back later as an optional view if it earns its place).
  • No outline column: sticky section headers plus a Jump to menu.
  • No inspector column: a step's settings open in place under it.
  • Calls between actions: a pink chip with Peek (expand in place, read-only) and Open (go in, with a breadcrumb trail back). The header shows "Used by", so you know who you affect when you change it.
  • Loops are an indented block with a yellow rule, so nesting reads at a glance.
