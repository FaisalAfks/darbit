Studio round 8b: an action editor for long actions

This is built for an action the size of ad-bot's: here, 400 steps in sections, waves and loops. Open the HTML and try it:

  • click a section in the outline to jump to it;
  • fold a group with ▾;
  • search steps;
  • Shift- or Ctrl-click to select many, then edit them together;
  • zoom the timeline with − / + (or Ctrl+wheel) and drag it, or its minimap;
  • drag the pane edges to resize them, and switch the list between compact and comfortable.

The list only draws the rows on screen, so 5 000 steps scroll as smoothly as 50. Sections are with d.step("Wave 1"): blocks in the saved Python, so the outline is the code's own structure.

What changed from round 8

  • Outline on the left: sections and loops with their step counts. Click to jump; the current one is highlighted while you scroll.
  • Timeline: lanes for keys, mouse, checks and waits, with the sections as bands on top. Zoom from the whole action down to single steps, drag to pan, and use the minimap to see where you are. Selected steps glow on it.
  • Step list: foldable groups, search, Shift/Ctrl multi-select, a drag grip to reorder, ＋ between rows to insert, compact or comfortable rows, breakpoints on hover. Only the rows on screen are drawn.
  • Inspector: tabs for Step, When (the screenshot from the moment it was recorded) and Python. With many steps selected it turns into bulk edit: speed up waits, wrap in a group or loop, turn into a reusable action, delete.
  • Panes resize by dragging their edges, and Focus hides the Studio's bars for the most room.
