What changed from K1

  • Magnifier: a rounded square that follows the mouse again, with no lag. It shows a 4× zoom with a pixel grid and a pixel marker, with the position and colour attached under it. It moves to the other side near an edge.
  • The selected box: drag it to move, drag any of 8 handles to resize, use the wheel to scale it around its centre, arrows to nudge it (Shift for 10 px), Ctrl+arrows to resize, drag the ✛ to move the click point (C centres it), and Ctrl+Z to undo. Hold ? for every key.
  • Test: only for the selected image. Its button glows while there's something to test. It searches the image's own region; the sweep shows where it looked. Matches get bracket boxes that snap in with their score, and matches under the line fade.
  • The name chip on the toolbar shows the region it belongs to. Type icons are on the type switch.
  • The guide card shows how the target looked for the bot's author, so you box the same thing.
  • The hint at the top left lists the keys, and fades once you've used them.
