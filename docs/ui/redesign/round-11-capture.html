One target is a picture, the area it's searched in, and where to click it. All three are made in one go, on the frozen screen, and saved together. ALayers on the shot

No modes to switch: the mouse does all three at once. Drag (or click) for the picture, Shift+drag for its area, Ctrl+click for the point. The pill's three chips show what the target has; click one to redo that part, right-click to drop it.

  • Fastest once known: a whole target in three gestures, as agreed in round 7.
  • Everything stays visible together, with editable readouts.
  • Shift and Ctrl have to be learned; the hint line says them.

BThree steps

The pill walks through Picture → Area → Point. Each step is one plain drag or click, and Area and Point can be skipped. Back on a done step redoes it; the last step's Next saves.

  • Nothing to learn: the pill says what to do next.
  • Good for setup guides and new users.
  • Slower for many targets; a fixed order.

CTool rail and target card

Like an image editor: a rail of tools on the left (select, picture, area, point; snap, magnifier pin, compare; undo, redo), the pill at the top for name, Test and Save, and a card with exact numbers, a saved area to reuse, and the history.

  • Every tool in sight, with its key; exact numbers always shown.
  • Room for more tools later (colour pick, a text read).
  • More on screen; the rail and card hide with the pill but take a corner while shown.
