Studio round 9: the Qt Studio, simplified

The v0.4 Studio grew two settings buttons, an Edit toggle in the run controls, a Customize button, and three rows of controls on top of the Library. This round keeps what works (bots front and centre, an easy way to make one, the Library's cards) and puts every control in one place. It also adds bots that run other bots, and a Library grouped by the screen each picture is on, not by the first part of its name.

1One shell: bots on the left, one bar on top

The bot menu becomes a sidebar that's always there: every bot with its state, the bots it runs folded under it, New bot at the bottom and darbit's own settings in the footer (the only ⚙ left). The top bar belongs to the chosen bot: its name, its four pages, and one control group. Play, Pause and Stop become one button that changes (Play while stopped; Pause and Stop while running), and Edit sits beside it as the only way into editing: it shows Capture, Record, Customize and Rename on the page you're on.

2Bots that run other bots

A bot can be made of other bots (ad-bot runs Raid, Speed Raid, Dimension and AFK, each its own bot with its own pictures and actions). In the sidebar they fold under the bot that runs them; on its Overview they're one list in the order they take turns, each with when it runs and an on/off switch. Picking one in the sidebar opens it like any bot: its own Library, Results and Settings. From there Play runs it alone (to test it), and the parent's Play runs the whole schedule.

3The Library by screen

Pictures are grouped by the screen they were taken on (Capture already keeps that screenshot), not by the first part of their name. The screens are a column on the left; the chosen one is shown with every picture, area and point in its place, and their cards below. Names stay whole (SR.Char.Sakura, not “Char.Sakura” under “SR”). A picture taken on no screen goes under Not on a screen; one can be moved to another screen by dragging its card. Search and the filters work across every screen.

4Every floating bar is the same pill

The run pill (the Studio hidden while a bot plays), the recorder and Capture's toolbar share one shape: a state on the left, the few controls that matter, and the main action last in the accent colour. Each shows a hint once, then only on hover.
