ARows in cards that fill the width

Like Windows 11's Settings: each setting one row, its words on the left and its control on the right. The section cards flow into two columns on a wide window and one on a narrow one. The section list has search and marks sections with changes.

  • Dense and calm: a whole section reads at a glance.
  • Uses the window's width without stretching any control.
  • Long help text is clamped to two lines (the rest on hover).

BTabs, rows and a help panel

Sections are tabs over the page; the rows carry only the name and the control. The setting under the mouse (or in focus) explains itself in the panel on the right: what it does, its value, default and range, and its key in settings.toml.

  • The cleanest rows; room for long explanations.
  • The key shown helps bot authors.
  • Reading a setting's help takes a hover; one column only.

CTiles in a grid

Each setting a tile, as many across as fit; the section names are chips to jump to. Sliders and long text take two tiles.

  • Most settings on screen at once; feels like a dashboard.
  • Matches the Library's card grid.
  • Short help only; uneven tile heights with long names.
