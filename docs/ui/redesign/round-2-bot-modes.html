Studio round 2: a bot you use, a bot you build

Tools now belong to the open bot; there's no separate Tools menu. The sidebar picks the bot and the centred bar picks its page. Run controls sit on the right. The difference is the mode. A bot you use guides you: take its missing pictures, fix a broken one. A bot you build is open: add assets, record actions. Three things to choose on this page.

Choice 1: how a bot gets its mode

AA Use / Build switch

adad-bot
UseBuild

Every bot has the switch in its header, and you can flip any bot to Build. Editing an installed bot changes your copy only; an update then asks what to keep.

BDecided by where the bot came from

Installed
adad-bot use
My projects
mymy-bot build

Installed bots are always Use, and your own project folders are always Build. To change an installed bot, Make my own copy turns it into a project. Updates never clash with your edits.

Choice 3: the line between Studio and bot

Two owners, never mixed. Tools keep nothing of their own: what they make goes into the bot, and how they behave is a Studio setting.

studio Yours, on this PC

How the Studio and its tools behave, for every bot. Open from ⚙ Studio.

%APPDATA%\darbit\
 ui.json  theme, hide on Run, tray, window size
 keymap.toml  your keys
 tools.toml  capture snap, zoom, recorder delays
%LOCALAPPDATA%\darbit\
 bots\  installed bots
 Cache\  thumbnails, safe to delete

bot The bot's, in its folder

Everything the bot needs and everything it produced. Copy the folder and the bot goes with it.

ad-bot\
 darbit.toml  name, version, what the Studio shows
 settings.toml  what can be set (the author's)
 config.toml  what you set (Settings page, not in git)
 assets\images\ regions.toml points.toml  Library: Capture writes here
 actions\  recorded and edited actions: Recorder writes here
 guide.toml  the setup guide
 .darbit\  runs, logs, screenshots, state (Results page, not in git)
Capture / Recorder / Highlight → write only into the open bot's folder. Their settings (snap, zoom) are in tools.toml, which belongs to the Studio.
