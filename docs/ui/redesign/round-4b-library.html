Studio round 4b: a richer Library

Built on A, with three improvements. The selection lifts and glows, gets a check mark, and the other cards fade. Each card shows more: its score against its similarity line, how often it's used, whether it has a search region, and a warning when it's weak. And C's on-the-screen view is built in. Two ways to do that last part:

1The screen in the inspector

The grid stays the main view. The inspector opens with the selected asset on its screen, glowing inside its search region. Below that are its numbers and its history. Click the preview to make it full size.
