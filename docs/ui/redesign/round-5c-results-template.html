Studio round 5c: Results as a template

The page is darbit's template, and each bot fills it in. The author lists what the bot tracks in darbit.toml: which gains lead the page, what totals to keep, and which modes get a timeline lane. The bot reports them with d.record. Below, the same template filled by ad-bot and by a fishing bot. The timeline is the whole day: zoom with − / + (or Ctrl+wheel), and drag it, or the box in the strip under it, left and right. Open the HTML to try it.

# darbit.toml: what the Results page shows [studio.results] gains = ["gems", "gold", "rewards", "rounds"] totals = ["weekly_characters", "raid_today", "best_gem_hour"] lanes = ["Raid", "Speed Raid", "AFK", "gems"] [studio.results.gems] label = "Gems" icon = "◆" sum = "gained" # adds up d.record("gems", gained=…) total = "balance" # and shows the last balance # in the bot's code: # d.record("gems", gained=120, balance=48210)

The gains are the big tiles and the day list numbers. The totals are the small table. The lanes are the timeline rows: modes (top-level steps) or recorded events.
